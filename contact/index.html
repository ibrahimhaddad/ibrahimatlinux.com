The fastest way to reach me, and the one I check most often. Include what the challenge is and what a good outcome looks like. The more specific you are, the faster I can tell you whether I am the right fit.

What to include for an advisory enquiry

Your organization and where it sits on the open source maturity curve, the specific problem, whether there is a deadline, and who else needs to be in the room. That is usually enough for me to say yes, no, or ask two questions.

3

What to include for a speaking invitation

Date, city, audience, format, and session length. If you have a topic in mind, the Speaking page lists what I cover.

Response time

Usually within two business days. I am selective about the engagements I take on, which means the ones I commit to get my full attention. If something is time-sensitive, say so up front.
